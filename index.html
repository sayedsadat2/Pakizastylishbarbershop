About us

Pakiza Stylish Barber Shop is a Whitby barber shop on Dundas Street West, built around one idea: everyone who sits in our chair leaves looking sharp and feeling good.

From a crisp fade to a hot towel shave and a tidy beard line, we take the time to get the details right. Dads, sons and grandfathers are all welcome.
